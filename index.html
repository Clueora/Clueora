Meet Clue

Your friendly AI research guide that points the way instead of giving the destination.

The problem with most homework apps

They hand kids the answer. We believe that teaches the wrong lesson.

❌

Answer dumping

Most AI tools just solve the problem. Kids copy, paste, and learn nothing about research or critical thinking.

🌐

Unreliable sources

Open web search can lead to random blogs, outdated pages, or even harmful content. Parents and teachers worry.

📉

Skill gap grows

Students who never practice finding and evaluating information struggle later in high school, college, and life.

How Clueora works

Three simple steps that turn homework into real learning.

Ask the question

Kids type or speak their homework question. Clueora asks a couple of clarifying questions about grade level and what they already know.

Get guided sources

We search only pre-vetted, high-credibility educational sites (Britannica, Khan Academy, museums, National Geographic Kids, etc.).

Follow the clues

Instead of the answer, kids receive step-by-step research prompts: “Look for the definition in the first paragraph… Now find an example…”

Built for real learning

Everything designed to help kids become better thinkers and researchers.
