# Clueora – Pitch One-Pager

**Lead kids to the answer. Never give it.**

---

### The Problem
Most AI homework tools simply hand students the final answer.  
Kids copy it, learn nothing, and miss the chance to build real research and critical-thinking skills.  
Parents and teachers are increasingly worried about both dependency on AI and exposure to unreliable internet sources.

### The Solution
**Clueora** is an AI research guide for students that:
- Never provides the final answer
- Searches only pre-vetted, high-credibility educational websites
- Gives smart clues and step-by-step research prompts
- Teaches kids *how* to find and understand information themselves

It turns homework into skill-building instead of answer-copying.

### How It Works
1. Student asks a homework question
2. Clueora searches a curated list of trusted sources (Britannica, Khan Academy, National Geographic Kids, museums, etc.)
3. Instead of the answer, the student receives guided clues and research prompts
4. Parents can see progress and sources used (without invading privacy)

### Why Now
- Explosive growth of AI homework tools that encourage shortcutting
- Rising demand from parents and schools for tools that actually teach
- Clear differentiation: almost every competitor gives the answer; we deliberately refuse to

### Target Users

- Parents who want real learning, not just completed homework
- Teachers looking for safe research tools
